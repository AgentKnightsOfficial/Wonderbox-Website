About us

The students behind WonderBox.

WonderBox is run by high school students who volunteer in Connecticut hospitals. After seeing how hard these stays can be on children, we decided to do something about it.

Our mission
WonderBox delivers free, age-tailored care packages to hospitalized children, bringing comfort, joy, and a reminder that they are not alone during a hard time.
