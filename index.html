the origin

Why we started WonderBox

A hospital stay is long, especially when you’re a kid. We wanted to make it a little easier.

Meet the team

We volunteer at hospitals around Connecticut, and one thing we all kept noticing was how bored and homesick the kids were, especially the ones staying more than a night or two. The doctors and nurses are focused on getting them better, which is exactly what they should be doing. But there isn’t always someone free to keep a ten-year-old company all afternoon.

So we started making care packages. We put together different boxes for young kids, preteens, and teens, because what helps a five-year-old pass the time isn’t what helps a sixteen-year-old. Everything is paid for & packed, so families never pay a thing.

So far, with a lot of help from our community, we’ve raised $3k+ and teamed up with three hospitals to get boxes to 120+ kids. It’s a small thing, but we hope it makes the day go by a little faster.

— the WonderBox team

How a WonderBox reaches a child

  1. step 1

    We gather

    We raise money and collect donated toys, games, books and comfort items.

  2. step 2

    We pack

    Each box is sorted and packed for one age group, then checked before it goes out.

  3. step 3

    We deliver

    Boxes go to pediatric patients at Connecticut Children's in Hartford and New Britain and at Yale New Haven Children's Hospital, at no cost to families.

  4. step 4

    A child opens it

    Someone who has spent days indoors gets something that is just for them.

pick a box

Three boxes for three ages

A four-year-old and a sixteen-year-old don't want the same thing. Tap a box to see what goes inside.

Ages 2 to 5

Soft, bright and easy to hold. Comfort comes first for the youngest patients.

  • Mini Play-Doh
  • 4-pack of crayons + coloring sheets
  • Board book or sticker book
  • Chunky puzzle or stacking cups
  • Bath toy or soft block
Ages 6 to 12

Things to build, draw and puzzle over, so long afternoons have something to do.

  • Crayons or colored pencils + coloring sheets
  • Word search
  • A pencil
  • Small LEGO-style set or card game
  • Fidget spinner, stress ball or worry stone
Ages 13 to 17

Made for teens, not scaled up from a little kid's box. Things that feel like they belong to them.

  • Puzzle book
  • Pen + a small journal
  • Fidget spinner, stress ball or worry stone
  • Deck of cards
  • Adult coloring book or small craft kit

Exact contents change with what donors and partners provide.
